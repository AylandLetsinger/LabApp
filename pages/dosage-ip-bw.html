Dosage Calculator

This calculator is to calculate the dosage of a drug or medication based on the patient or animal's weight.

Step 1. Select delivery method:

Use the buttons below to select the specific type of dosage calculator that you need, then follow the steps within.

  • Stock Solution
  • Direct Application
  • Intraperitoneal Injection
  • Subcutaneous Injection
  • Intracranial Injection/Infusion
  • Intravenous Injection
  • Drinking Fluid
  • Eatable Solid

Step 2. Select Dosage Method

Use the buttons below to select the method to calculate dosage that you need, then follow the steps within.

  • Dose Per Subject
  • Dose By Body Weight
  • Dose By Solution Concentration
  • Dose by Infusion Rate

Step 3. Enter Study Parameters:

Enter the study parameters below to calculate the dosage by body weight for an IP injection. Parameters you must enter are highlighted in orange. Change the units using the dropdown menus.
